Sizes 8..40 step 0.25 (129 per font), three fonts: Liberation Serif Regular, DejaVu Sans, Liberation Sans Regular. line-height normal.

Ascent-to-baseline == round(asc) on 368/387; the 19 misses are all Liberation Serif at 35.5..40 px inclusive (step 0.25, 19 sizes; earlier text said 39 because the printed list was cut at 15 rows) where Chrome's ascent is 1 more than round(asc) (block height still matches). So the line-box height rule has now held on a dense grid in three fonts; the ascent rule has a known counterexample.
