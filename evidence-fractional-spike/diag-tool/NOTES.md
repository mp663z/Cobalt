Reproduces the earlier grid: 1173 cases, 1156 identical, 17 flips (Liberation Serif 1, DejaVu 7, Liberation Sans 9).
CORRECTION of the earlier margin metric: the flip classification must use the margin of the first decision where Chrome and the model differ (|decision_slack| of the word that the earlier-breaking side rejected), not the model's own rejected-word overflow at its first differing break. With that metric ALL 17 flips have margin <= 0.2215 px; the Liberation Sans 12 px / 160.109375 px case is NOT structural: Chrome breaks before 'jumping' where the model fits it with 0.0254 px to spare (Chrome line 114.765625 + space 3.34375 + word 42.03125 = 160.140625 > 160.109375; model 160.084 <= 160.109). The independent reviewer's 'structural' label for this case used a different overflow metric (9.297 px, own recomputation not reproduced by me); the two metrics should be reconciled by the reviewer.
Direction: in 16 of 17 flips the model breaks and Chrome fits (Chrome tolerates more), in 1 (Sans 12 px) Chrome breaks and the model fits. A single sign-consistent width bias therefore does not explain them.
Empirical rule on this grid only (diag-margin-rule.txt): flagging cases with any word-fit margin < 0.25 px catches 17/17 flips and flags 44 correct cases (3.8% of correct, 5.2% of all); < 0.10 catches 14/17 with 1.0% over-refusal. No bound is claimed for other strings, sizes, faces or widths.
