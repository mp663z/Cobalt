Field notes on paper screens

An e-ink panel holds its image without power, which makes it good for reading and poor for animation. The refresh policy decides how often the whole panel flashes.

A short history

The first readers used electrophoretic panels with sixteen greys. Later models added colour filters, and a few went back to plain black and white.
